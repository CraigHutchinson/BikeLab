## Findings that change the plan

1. **Current-plan status was stale.** The roadmap and issue plan still said twelve drafts awaited integration, #40 awaited browser receiving and the rider stack carried obsolete CI guards. #80/#81 and main's CI restoration supersede those statements. D5's headline also omitted portable riders and active controller work. The current sections are updated; dated measurement receipts remain historical.
2. **Feature completion exceeds qualification.** Both-bike UI checks completed, but 12/22 strict rider comparisons fail and all eight moving crouches fail transient tracking. #81 identifies a sufficient target-table cause in two short cases only. Extend identical-input diagnosis to longer/controller cases, then version target identity before reference-rate feedback. Preserve legacy mechanics and the existing comparison/tracking budgets.
3. **Rest requires a physical and numerical contract.** New #77 reports substantial parked-bike creep under regularized brakes; #79 reports contact-point ambiguity. Reproduce both before treating a held brake as exact lock or a single contact realization as a unique physical reference. Pair contact law and integrator controls; do not tune controller gains or damping to conceal an unresolved support mechanism.
4. **Smoothing needs its own admission and refinement gates.** #82/#83 extend the continuous-edge proposal: fixed candidate sets, corner-gradient continuity, a recorded numerical band and adequate broad-phase margin. Verify the chosen formulation's gradient and gap bound locally; the port's `2*band` bound and example widths are formulation-specific, not universal constants or tyre material properties. Cross timestep and band independently to distinguish integration convergence from changing the model.
5. **Broader delivery must remain visible.** Finite saddle support/retention, persistent session/advance with bounded recording, live ownership, calibration, 3D handling, layered terrain and measured real-time budgets remain unreceived. After replay identity, the persistent-session contract can progress on unchanged mechanics independently of contact research; do not defer all product execution work until every tyre issue is closed.
