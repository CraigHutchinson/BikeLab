# Explicit chassis construction without an implied rider

This small extraction follows the [rider observer](RIDER_UNBOUND_OBSERVER.md), received and merged in [PR32](https://github.com/CraigHutchinson/BikeLab/pull/32). It makes native chassis construction usable without inserting a rider or interpreting historical combined bike/rider mass as chassis mass. Existing rig and Trail browser/worker/headless consumers retain their models and records. It adds no shared-world riding mode.

## Contract and compatibility

`bikeChassisAssembly(p, spatial, chassis, frameContents = '')` in [bike-assembly.js](../dist/bike-assembly.js) requires explicit chassis `mass`, `com: [x, z]` and `inertia: [Ix, Iy, Iz]`. These are own enumerable data fields. COM is measured in the frame's local coordinates, in metres; the diagonal inertia is central at that COM, in kg m², expressed along the frame axes. The XML must not add a parallel-axis shift to those values. Native compilation may permute principal axes without changing the physical tensor.

`p` supplies previously validated bike geometry and suspension parameters. The builder never reads legacy `mass`, `rider` or `riderMass` fields. Optional spatial wheel and frame-content strings are trusted, internally generated native fragments, not a user XML importer. Empty frame contents create no rider, attachment sites or equalities. Native names remain reserved to one bike assembly; this is not yet a multiple-bike naming API.

[bike-chassis.js](../dist/bike-chassis.js) owns the illustrative 6 kg chassis defaults and the existing mass/COM/inertia bounds and triangle inequalities. `validateChassis({})` expands defaults and returns copied, frozen vectors. Explicit `undefined` is rejected. The connected rider adapter retains its existing nested schema, optional defaults, bounds and error behavior. The portable setup schema still keeps chassis properties under `bikeMount.chassis`; its expanded legacy scenario still uses `articulated.chassis`.

The existing `bikeAssembly` compatibility wrapper retains the fixed/guided combined-mass partition and its exact arithmetic. Its articulated path passes explicit chassis properties and the same generated rider sites/body into the new builder. Front/rear tree order, inertial XML, shock sites/tendon and historical geometry offsets are preserved. Spatial wheels replace the lumped axle masses. With current defaults, carriers plus hubs plus tyres contribute 7 kg, so the riderless spatial bike totals 13 kg, independently of any rider mass. These masses and inertias remain illustrative and uncalibrated.

This preserves existing replay rather than changing a model identifier.

Independent coordinate transforms check native chassis/carrier and total COM. All nine entries of the world central inertia tensor are reconstructed and checked. Lumped and real deformable-wheel configurations have the same independently summed 16 kg bike mass, with no implicit rider joints, sites or equality rows. Throwing getters prove that the builder never reads legacy rider or combined-mass fields.

A construction-only fixture places the existing 70 kg rider under a separate world root beside each bike. Total mass is 86 kg; moving the bike does not move the rider trunk, and bike fit sites remain owned by the frame. This checks topology and kinematic ownership, not coupled dynamics, reciprocal independence, detachment or lifetime.

## Next bounded integration

The user's [active stability requirement](RIDER_ACTIVE_STABILITY.md) adds a parallel priority: sustained idle, controlled COM transfer and recovery over consecutive bumps. The passive baseline reaches an elbow guard near 0.12 s on both bikes. A bounded active posture-hold slice can be received on the attached model while the independent-root work below proceeds; separate controller ownership and explicit effort/work remain required.

Introduce a versioned shared-world Trail case with disjoint rider/bike roots, both bike types and actor-scoped observations. Receive separate and combined mass, COM, momentum and energy; reciprocal independence controls; correct gravity moments over the participating body set; full-world rollback; explicit unsupported collision/separation boundaries; fresh identity and deterministic replay. Keep the old models and attached-rider release guard unchanged until the new mode owns these responsibilities. A construction fixture alone is insufficient.

Then receive supported saddle/contact behavior, active posture and the [pump, wheelie, hop and endo controls](RIDER_MANOEUVRES.md). Preserve finite suspension/casing domains and explicit overload stops; they do not predict fracture or tubeless pressure loss. Calibrated component failure, faceted impact refinement, layered soil/mud, 3D lateral/turning dynamics, persistent execution and measured multirate real-time qualification remain required. Lower-rate rider intent/control must not freeze mechanical rider state during a fast impact; see the [multirate contract](MULTIRATE_EXECUTION.md).
