## Small executable scope

Deliver one terrain-request station with top view and selectable longitudinal/transverse cutaway, plus the same pure request/extraction module in a Node replay script. Prescribe axle/carrier poses on straight, left/right turn, stationary-steer and reverse tracks. Show a finite-width wheel envelope, required Hc coverage, optional Hp ribbons, resident tile edges, missing cells, sample columns, rock faces and outward normals. Display identities, counts and cache events. Scrubbing reads saved geometry; waiting for required data visibly holds the next simulation tick. Data acquisition never creates forces.

Use a small immutable synthetic terrain atlas, gravity-aligned local T with x forward, y left, z up, metres and seconds, and an explicit translation/yaw T-to-W transform. Implement analytic plane/ramp and rectilinear layered/step fixtures rather than raster/network import or arbitrary mesh loading. Keep finite y width even when the initial track is straight. A full 3D scene renderer is optional; 3D coordinates and side-face geometry are required. This slice does not require a universal source/provider API, material solver, deformation store, probabilistic predictor, multibody change or state-transfer framework.

The authored layered fixture has soil top z=0.02 m, rock crown -0.03 m before x=6 and +0.05 m after x=6, rock bottom -0.20 m, and finite lateral rock extent. Soil occupancy is removed wherever rock occupies the volume. Retain the exposed and buried vertical face with normal (-1,0,0); do not interpolate the step into a ramp. Add a resolved off-centre root body or rock edge across one lateral row, plus a labelled soil/root-reinforcement material region. These demonstrate distinct geometry versus reinforcement metadata only; they supply no stiffness, strength or support force. Unknown below the fixture depth remains unknown.

## Conservative Hc and optional Hp

For prescribed tracks, bound every intervening pose analytically. A practical conservative implementation is the union of interval boxes of the transformed tyre interaction envelope, inflated by proven translation/rotation bounds over each subdivision; an envelope bounding sphere can conservatively cover unsampled carrier rotation. Store the enclosing bound and subdivision/error rule. Pose samples alone are not a sweep certificate. A conservative box union may fetch extra cells; optimization is unnecessary for R0. Required vertical coverage includes finite admitted sinkage/substrate depth and the full relevant feature halo, rather than only the surface raster.

Hc covers the entire next accepted dt and full fore/aft reach, tyre width, tolerance, lookbehind and admitted motion. At zero speed it retains a footprint and steering sweep. Reverse motion changes lead direction. Use actual prescribed displacement, not heading times a guessed forward speed. Before advancing, verify the whole required cell/feature set and requested depth/capabilities against the matching leased response. A known hole and an unknown cell remain different. Missing required data holds the tick; outside-domain or unsupported reports a visible stop. No endpoint-only or rectangle-only admission when internal holes exist.

Hp extends a separately labelled optional route footprint. Use recorded illustrative route polylines/width profiles, with a straight narrow/long case, a widened asymmetric turn/bump case and mirrored routes. No Gaussian or confidence calibration is needed. Their union is expanded by the finite wheel envelope; overlapping routes count once. Weights affect optional fetch priority only. Include an admitted excursion outside the optional ribbon but inside Hc to prove that prefetch probability never clips required coverage. If the queue/cap is tight, drop optional tiles first; never return complete after clipping mandatory width or depth.

## Cache, async and leases

Key immutable source entries by asset/content revision and canonical tile/feature ID; deduplicate front/rear fetches. Keep source residency separate from per-request extraction. Repeated extraction while moving within resident tiles must produce hits without reloading. A turn that crosses the old patch boundary loads its neighbor and features, rather than clamping to an old edge.

An injected asynchronous fixture loader provides delayed, failed and out-of-order delivery under a deterministic event schedule. Browser workers are optional for this bounded station, but promise completion must exercise real publication races. Requests capture generation/sequence/revisions. Revalidate all identities at publication and consumption. A stale result may populate an identical immutable source entry, but cannot replace the active extract. Reset increments generation; cancellation is best effort. Revision mismatch, cancellation and failure remain distinct recorded outcomes.

Issue an explicit lease for every published read view. Pin its source buffers until release; eviction may select only unpinned entries. Do not transfer/detach buffers or reuse extraction storage while a lease is live. Each response owns its extraction arrays until released. Under all-pinned pressure return pending/budget exhaustion and hold; do not mutate a reader. Release superseded/stale results in every completion branch. Bound outstanding requests and extract leases as well as source bytes, because a source-cache limit alone does not bound retained output memory.

Start with fixture limits no larger than PR #8's 4096 columns, 16384 spans, 16 spans/column, 128 features, 32768 triangles, 32 MiB source and eight queued requests; also specify max live leases/output bytes. For a smaller implemented subset, record tighter caps. No duration threshold or real-time claim follows from these counts.

## Receiving checks

Headless checks use independent analytic or hand-authored expectations, not a second call to the extractor: plane heights/normals, ramp normal proportional to (-a,-b,1), exact step incident faces without a blended normal, ordered nonoverlapping spans, buried/piercing rock occupancy, finite-width off-centre edge and known-depth limits. Verify tile seams against an untiled fixture with canonical IDs/features deduplicated; unknown/nodata cells never interpolate across their hole. Test both sides of cell and tile boundaries and finite z coverage. Corner/centre placement tests apply to the declared synthetic grid convention; raster import remains deferred.

Request/cache checks cover straight reuse, turn-triggered fetch, mirrored requests, zero-speed rotation, reverse/lookbehind, low-probability excursion, mandatory versus optional budget exhaustion, queue limits, out-of-order sequences, reset/cancel, source revision change, failed load, pinned eviction, release/reuse and an old reader remaining unchanged after a newer request. Save and replay availability events at simulation ticks; preload replay or the saved schedule must reproduce accepted poses, holds, samples, features and identities independently of wall-clock ordering.

Browser checks operate the actual station: presets, run/pause/reset, scrub, cuts and lateral-row selection, forced missing-data wait, stale request reset, export/import and a narrow mobile viewport. Compare exported consumed arrays/identities and accepted tick sequence with the headless run. Inspect captures showing off-centre geometry, both Hc/Hp overlays, an exposed face/cutaway and explicit missing coverage. A failed capture is not visual evidence. Preserve existing station checks and run the project suite after implementation.
