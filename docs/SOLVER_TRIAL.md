# Prismatic solver trial

This is the first executable arm of [the solver comparison](SOLVER_RESEARCH.md), following [the iteration 5 baseline review](ITERATION_5_REVIEW.md). It does not select a bicycle solver. The static `solver.html` viewer, cancellable module worker and `node scripts/trial-solver.mjs [setup.json]` all consume the same implementation. CLI exit codes are 0 for all implemented gates passing, 2 for a completed comparison with unmet gates or rejected candidate continuation, and 1 for invalid input or execution/I/O failure. Standard output is the complete JSON report; setup files are limited to 16 KiB.

## Mechanism and reference

A fixed body anchors a dynamic rigid carriage through an actual 3D prismatic impulse joint. No body translation or rotation locks conceal constraint drift. No collider/contact participates. The carriage has the selected mass, COM at its origin, and illustrative positive spherical principal inertia [1,1,1] kg m². Its identity orientation is constrained by the joint. Sleeping and body damping are disabled. The rail allows one independent displacement; the other five relative body DOFs are constrained.

Axes are x forward, y left, z up. For tilt theta from vertical, d=(sin(theta),0,-cos(theta)), n=(cos(theta),0,sin(theta)); body position starts at d*q0 and velocity at d*u0. Positive compression q=d·position; u=d·velocity. A constant transverse force F*n loads the constraint at the COM. An optional torque probe applies the selected N m magnitude about the fixed unit (1,2,3) axis, exercising all three constrained rotations without altering the ideal axial equation. Gravity is [0,0,-9.80665] m/s². Spring preload+k*q and viscous c*u oppose positive compression. `ForceBased` with target -preload/k supplies these physical coefficients. `AccelerationBased` would rescale by effective mass and represent a different equation.

The fixed-base reference solves m*q''=m*g*cos(theta)-preload-k*q-c*q'. Define qeq=(m*g*cos(theta)-preload)/k and e=q-qeq; the existing independently tested closed-form oscillator evaluates e,u from the authored initial state. The reference receives no candidate state. The passive-energy displacement envelope sqrt((q0-qeq)²+(u0/sqrt(k/m))²) must remain at least 1 mm inside [0,travel]. This sufficient admission test is conservative and does not model end stops. Any candidate endpoint outside stroke rejects the trial. No within-step impact guarantee, checkpoint rollback or physically supported continuation is provided; these remain separate stop gates.

Compression/velocity and reconstructed constitutive force are compared at the **engine's stored float32 timestep** times the integer tick. The authored dt remains in params; engine.storedDt and effectiveSampleDt disclose its rounding. The intended recording interval is 5 ms; all permitted integration steps divide it. Duration is 0.1–2 s, at most 64,000 outer steps (256,000 internal substeps in the original world) and 401 recorded samples. The restored world runs alongside the original after halfway; these are offline calculations.

The force channel evaluates preload+k*q+c*u at recorded states. Rapier solves its motor implicitly inside the constraint solve; the sampled constitutive value is **not** its actual impulse divided by a step. Actual joint reaction wrench/impulse is unavailable through this binding. Neither a force error pass nor small joint drift satisfies the attachment-reaction or energy/work qualification gate.

## Gates and restart evidence

Reference maximum limits remain 1e-6 m, 1e-4 m/s and 0.1 N. Rail-position residual is |position-d*q|; angular closure is the shortest quaternion rotation from identity. Their every-step maxima are limited to 1e-5 m/rad. Recorded force/reference comparisons and every-step closure/travel checks have different sampling scopes. RMS values are descriptive, not an alternate pass rule.

At halfway, take the engine snapshot, restore a separate world, reacquire the carriage handle and compare all position/velocity/quaternion/angular-velocity components at every subsequent step. Compare complete final snapshot bytes as well. Application state in this fixture consists of immutable setup and the integer tick; there are no external histories. No contact warm-start, soil, cross-version migration or continuous live tier-swap claim follows. Snapshot work and the extra world are included in the offline experiment, never described as real-time cost.

The viewer exposes failed gates without stopping ordinary replay. Editing invalidates transport/export until a fresh run completes; cancellation terminates the worker. Save exports params, artifact identity, solver settings, traces, metrics and limitations. The file is evidence, not an imported trusted engine snapshot; rerun its params to reproduce it.

Browser/Node checks require exact candidate channels and metadata for the tested pinned pair. Analytical transcendental evaluations and derived error reductions use a separately declared 1e-12 normalized tolerance because measured JS-runtime differences were ~1e-17 m and ~1e-13 N. This does not change the candidate-versus-reference accuracy limits. Same-runtime repeat/restart remains exact.

## Timestep reversal diagnosis

The [source-backed investigation](TIMESTEP_RESEARCH.md) reproduces the finest-step reversal and separates early split external-force/motor velocity rounding from late absolute-position rounding. Equal internal h reproduces axial traces despite different outer-step grouping; more PGS passes do not resolve the finest error. Diagnostic force balancing and coordinate centring together restore refinement for the tested fixture, but change the attachment-force representation and are not substituted into the production trial. No accuracy limits or force laws change. Use `node scripts/diagnose-timestep.mjs` and `node scripts/timestep-scalar.mjs` for the matched engine and independent arithmetic controls.
